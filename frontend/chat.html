Choose how to use location

Sharing your location helps find nearby places and calculate straight-line distances. Your browser will ask for GPS permission only after you choose “Share my location”.

With consent, precise coordinates are saved in this browser and sent to this site's backend. The backend uses them for TomTom place search, OpenWeather, and Safeswim. Exact coordinates are not included in the Gemini or NVIDIA prompt; the AI does receive the nearby candidate place names and weather, which can imply an approximate area. When a result map is shown, your browser also requests OpenStreetMap tiles for the displayed area.

You can use Auckland Central instead, or choose/adjust a starting point on the separate Explore map.
