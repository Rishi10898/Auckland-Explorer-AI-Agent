SOUTH AUCKLAND COASTLINE

Explore South Auckland beaches.

Discover South Auckland’s varied coast, from sheltered Hauraki Gulf bays along the Pohutukawa Coast to the tidal Manukau Harbour and wild Tasman Sea shores of Franklin and Awhitu Peninsula. Check tides, water quality, and local safety advice before swimming; west-coast surf and currents can be hazardous.
